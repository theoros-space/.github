# Licensing at Theoros

Every Theoros repository is public. Two of them are open source; the others
are source-available.

| Repository | License | In short |
|---|---|---|
| theoros-models | Theoros Open License 1.0 | Open source: code and trained weights, any use, patent grant, name and logo protected |
| theoros-edge | Theoros Source-Available 1.0 | Read and evaluate; use needs a written license |
| theoros-delivery | Theoros Source-Available 1.0 | Read and evaluate; use needs a written license |
| theoros-ground | Theoros Source-Available 1.0 | Read and evaluate; use needs a written license |
| theoros-bench | Apache License 2.0 | Open source, free to use and modify |
| theoros-docs | CC BY 4.0 | Free to share and adapt with credit |
| theoros-brand | Theoros Brand Terms | Use unmodified to refer to Theoros |
| .github | CC BY 4.0 | Free to share and adapt with credit |

Why the model is open: TheorosNet is developed with EuroHPC open R&D
resources, and an open model lets agencies and operators inspect, test and
qualify exactly what would fly. The Theoros Open License 1.0 is our own
license; it allows any use and adds a patent grant and protection of the
Theoros name.

Why edge, delivery and ground are source-available: customers, agencies and
investors can inspect the flight runtime and the delivery chain, while the
commercial product stays protected.

Commercial, research and flight licenses: licensing@theoros.space.

Before code is published, check with the CTO whether anything in it should
first be protected by a patent filing. Publishing an invention can prevent
patenting it later.

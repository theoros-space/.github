# Code of Conduct

Theoros adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, critique work and not people, and
keep discussions focused on building the best possible product.

Report unacceptable behaviour to conduct@theoros.space. Reports are handled
confidentially by the leadership team.
